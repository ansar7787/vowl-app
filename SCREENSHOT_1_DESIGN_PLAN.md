# 🎨 Vowl: Screenshot 1 Design Translation Plan

This document explains exactly how to take the "Grocery" template you found and adapt it perfectly into the **First Play Store Screenshot (The Hook)** for your English learning app, Vowl.

---

## 📱 1. What goes inside the Mobile Frame?

**Do not use a random or boring screen!** 
Inside the phone frame, you must put the **Main Dashboard / Learning Map** of your app. 
*   **Why?** The phone frame in the template says "Good Morning!". Your dashboard likely has the Vowl Mascot greeting the user or showing their current level/map. This creates a welcoming first impression and immediately shows them that the app is gamified.

---

## 🎨 2. The Color Palette (Replacing Orange & Beige)

The template uses Orange and Beige. We need to change these to match Vowl's premium, gamified brand.

*   **Top Jagged Header (Replace the Orange):**
    *   Use your brand's **Neon Green (`#A8E063`)**. This makes it instantly recognizable as Vowl.
*   **Bottom Background (Replace the Beige):**
    *   **Option A (Clean & Modern):** Use a very light, soft mint/white color like **`#F0FDF4`**. This keeps the clean look of the template but ties into your green branding.
    *   **Option B (Premium Dark Mode):** Use a deep, rich navy blue like **`#0F172A`**. The neon green header will "pop" incredibly hard against a dark background, making it look like a high-end video game. *(I highly recommend Option B!)*
*   **The "Add to Cart" Button color inside the phone:**
    *   Since this is just a screenshot of your app, whatever the primary button color is in your app (likely green or blue) will be captured automatically!

---

## ✍️ 3. The Text Replacements

You need to replace the grocery text with high-converting marketing copy.

**Top Section (Replacing "FRESHLY PICKED Groceries"):**
*   **Replace the small pill ("FRESHLY PICKED"):** Type `YOUR ENGLISH`
*   **Replace the massive text ("Groceries"):** Type `Adventure` (Use a thick, bold, playful font like *Outfit* or *Lilita One*).

**Bottom Section (Replacing "Our Promise" & the paragraph):**
*   **Replace "Our Promise":** Type `Learn by Playing`
*   **Replace the paragraph:** Type: `Join the ultimate gamified learning experience. Practice speaking fearlessly, fix grammar with the ballista, and climb the elite leaderboards!`

**Top Right Circular Stamp (Replacing the Grocery Stamp):**
*   Change the text inside the circle to say: `⭐ 100% FREE AI TUTOR ⭐ VOWL APP`
*   Replace the paper bag icon in the center of the stamp with a **Star** or a **Crown**.

---

## 🍎 4. The Floating Icons (Replacing the Fruits)

You cannot have strawberries and pineapples floating around an English app! Replace them with 3D educational and gaming icons.

**What to use instead:**
1.  **Bottom Right (Replace Pineapple):** A massive, shiny **3D Gold Coin** or a **Treasure Chest** (to represent the XP and rewards they earn).
2.  **Top Right (Replace Pear):** A **3D Microphone** (to represent the Speaking and Roleplay feature).
3.  **Left Side (Replace Strawberry):** A **3D Target Board with an Arrow** (to represent the Ballista Grammar game).
4.  **Small Floating Dots (Replace Cherries):** Small **Stars** or **Alphabet Blocks** (A, B, C).

---

## 🛠️ 5. How to build this quickly

If you are using Canva or Figma:
1. Keep the exact layout (the jagged line separating the top and bottom).
2. Change the top color to `#A8E063` and the bottom color to `#0F172A`.
3. Go to the "Elements" tab and search for "3D Gold Coin" and "3D Microphone" to replace the fruits.
4. Drop the screenshot of your Main Map into the center phone frame.
5. Update the text.

This layout is incredibly professional, and by applying Vowl's colors and 3D gaming icons, it will look like a AAA studio designed it!
