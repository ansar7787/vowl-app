### 💡 Pro-Tip for Seamless Swiping
Because these 4 layouts have the "jagged line" separating the two colors, you can align them so the jagged line connects when the user swipes from Screenshot 1 to Screenshot 2! This makes the Play Store gallery look like one massive, connected piece of art.
