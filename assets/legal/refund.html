REFUND POLICY

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. GENERAL POLICY

All in-app purchases in Vowl — including Premium passes and Coin Packs — are digital goods. Once a purchase is successfully completed and the digital content is delivered to your account, the purchase is considered final.

Summary: Purchases are non-refundable after delivery, except in the specific cases listed below.

2. ELIGIBLE REFUND SCENARIOS

We will issue a refund in the following situations:

  • Failed Delivery: Payment was deducted from your account, but Premium was not activated or coins were not credited due to a technical error.
  • Duplicate Charge: You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • Unauthorised Purchase: A purchase was made without your consent (e.g., by a child without parental authorisation). You must report this within 48 hours of the transaction.

3. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS

The following are not eligible for refund:

  • Premium passes where access has been used (even partially).
  • Coin Packs where coins have been spent (even partially).
  • Purchases made more than 48 hours ago (unless they fall under Section 2).
  • "Change of mind" after purchase — you agreed to the purchase terms at checkout.

4. GOOGLE PLAY PURCHASES

If your purchase was made through Google Play Billing, Google's own refund policies apply:

  • Google Play offers a 48-hour refund window for most in-app purchases.
  • You can request a refund directly through Google Play: Google Play Order History.
  • After 48 hours, refund requests for Google Play purchases should be directed to us.

5. RAZORPAY PURCHASES (INDIA)

If your purchase was made through Razorpay:

  • Automatic refund for failed transactions: If your payment is deducted but the purchase fails to activate (due to a network error or processing failure), Razorpay automatically detects the dropped transaction and issues a refund to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Manual refund requests: For eligible scenarios (Section 2), contact us with your account email and Razorpay Transaction ID.

6. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

To request a refund for an eligible scenario:

  • Step 1: Email us at support.vowl.app@gmail.com
  • Step 2: Include your registered email address, Transaction ID, and a description of the issue.
  • Step 3: We will review your request within 2 business days.

Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.

7. ABUSE PREVENTION

We reserve the right to deny refund requests that show a pattern of abuse, including but not limited to:

  • Repeatedly purchasing and requesting refunds.
  • Using premium features and then requesting a refund.
  • Exploiting the refund process to gain free access.

Accounts found to be abusing the refund system may be suspended or terminated.
