# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you create an account:

- **Account Data:** Name, email address, and profile picture (via email registration or Google Sign-In).
- **Age Group:** Whether you are an adult or under 16, to provide the appropriate experience.
- **Learning Data:** Progress, quest completion, game scores, streaks, XP, and coin balances.
- **Device Data:** Device identifiers, operating system version, and crash reports for improving app stability.

We do **not** collect voice recordings, GPS location, contacts, or phone numbers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To create and manage your account.
- To personalise your language learning experience and track quest progress.
- To manage gamification rewards (coins, XP, streaks, trophies).
- To process in-app purchases securely.
- To send important service updates and optional learning reminders (which you can disable).
- To analyse usage trends, fix technical issues, and improve the app via crash reports.

## 3. Advertising and Analytics

Our app includes optional third-party advertisements via **Google AdMob**. Ad networks may collect device identifiers to provide relevant ads. We use Google's UMP (User Messaging Platform) to collect your consent before serving personalised ads, in compliance with GDPR.

- **Adults with consent:** Personalised ads may be shown.
- **Adults without consent:** Only non-personalised ads are shown.
- **Kids Zone users (under 16):** No personalised ads are ever shown.
- **Premium users:** All ads are removed entirely.

We use **Firebase Analytics** to understand how the app is used. You can opt out of analytics collection in Settings.

## 4. In-App Purchases & Payments

Vowl offers optional Premium passes and Coin Packs:

- **India:** Payments are processed securely by **Razorpay**.
- **Other countries:** Payments are processed via **Google Play Billing**.

We do **not** collect or store your credit card, UPI, or bank information on our servers.

## 5. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **Firebase Authentication:** Secure user sign-in.
- **Cloud Firestore:** Secure, encrypted database for your learning data.
- **Firebase Storage:** Profile picture storage (size-limited to 5MB, images only).
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** Crash reports to fix bugs (no personal data included).
- **Firebase Analytics:** Usage analytics (opt-out available in Settings).
- **Google AdMob:** Advertising (consent-based).
- **Razorpay / Google Play Billing:** Payment processing.

## 6. Data Security

- Firestore security rules ensure users can only read/write their own data.
- Storage rules validate file size (max 5MB) and type (images only) for uploads.
- Rate limiting prevents abuse of write operations.
- All API communications use HTTPS encryption.
- Debug information is stripped from release builds.

## 7. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**. Upon deletion:

- All personal data is permanently removed from our databases.
- All scheduled notifications are cancelled.
- Local preferences are cleared from your device.

This action is irreversible.

## 8. Children's Privacy (Kids Zone)

Vowl includes a dedicated **Kids Zone** for users under 16:

- **No personalised ads** are ever shown to children.
- **Parental gate** (math challenge) is required before any purchase.
- **Minimal data collection:** Only educational progress data is collected.
- Children cannot access adult features or make unsupervised purchases.
- We comply with COPPA and equivalent regulations.

If we learn we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.

## 9. Your Rights

- **GDPR (European users):** Right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data.
- **DPDPA (Indian users):** Right to access, correct, or erase your data.
- **All users:** You can delete your account at any time from within the app.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Effective Date" above.
