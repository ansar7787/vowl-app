# 🎨 Vowl: The Ultimate 8-Screenshot Master Design Guide

I have updated this guide to remove words like "Ballista" or "Jigsaw". We are focusing purely on the actual English learning mechanics of your app (Fill in the blanks, Drag and Drop words, etc.) while keeping the marketing text highly engaging!

**Rule of Thumb for Text Contrast:**
*   If the background is a dark color (like Navy Blue), the text **MUST be Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**.
*   If the background is a bright, light color (like Neon Green or Yellow), the text **MUST be very dark (like `#0F172A`)**.
